Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Kreevo ("we", "us") makes tools for video editors: MotionType, CreatorStash, Ultimate SFX Pack and Crash Guard, sold through Gumroad, and the Instagram account @kreevo.assets. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

1. Instagram messaging (Kreevo Bot)

When you comment on a post of @kreevo.assets or send us a direct message, our automation ("Kreevo Bot", which uses the official Instagram API from Meta) may:

  • reply to your comment and send you one direct message with the link you asked for;
  • check whether your account follows @kreevo.assets, only after you tap the "I followed" button;
  • remember which product you asked about and that we already answered you, so you don't get duplicate messages.

For this we process your Instagram-scoped user ID, the text of your comment or message, and the follow status returned by Instagram. We do not see your password, we do not read your other conversations, and we never sell or share this data. It is stored on Cloudflare servers and deleted automatically after at most 30 days.

2. Purchases and licenses

Payments are processed by Gumroad. We receive your name, email, country and order details from Gumroad to deliver the product, send license keys and answer support requests. We never see your card details. Product panels check a license key with our licensing service; they send only the key and a device identifier, never your projects or files.

3. Website

kreevo.github.io is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not use cookies or trackers of our own. The live MotionType demo runs entirely in your browser.

4. Your rights and data deletion

To delete your data, email kreevoassets@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Instagram username or purchase email. We delete everything we hold about you within 7 days and confirm by email. You can also simply stop messaging us: bot data expires on its own after 30 days.

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you or to correct it.
