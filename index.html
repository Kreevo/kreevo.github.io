How do I install After Effects scripts? +
Copy the .jsx file to: C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects [version]\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\ — then restart AE and find it under the Window menu.
Do I get free updates? +
Yes — all products auto-update via GitHub. Just click "Check Update" inside the panel and it installs automatically.
What After Effects version do I need? +
AE CC 2019 and above. Tested on AE 2025.
