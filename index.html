FAQ
Questions?
Can I try before I buy? +
Yes. Try MotionType live on this page with your own text, and listen to sounds from the Ultimate SFX Pack.
Mac or Windows? +
Both. All Kreevo tools work on macOS and Windows.
Does MotionType work in Premiere Pro? +
Yes. Since version 2.1 the MotionType panel works in both After Effects (2019 or newer) and Premiere Pro (2021 or newer) — one installer, one license key — and all 26 animations also come as Motion Graphics templates. CreatorStash works in both apps too, and the Ultimate SFX Pack works in any editor: Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut and more.
How many computers can I use? +
A MotionType license key works on up to 3 computers. It needs internet once to activate, then works offline.
How do I install After Effects scripts? +
Every product comes with an INSTALL.txt guide. For panels like CreatorStash and MotionType just run the included one-click installer (.bat on Windows, .command on Mac), restart After Effects / Premiere Pro, then open it from Window → Extensions.
Do I get free updates? +
Yes — updates are free forever. The panels tell you when a new version is ready.
What After Effects version do I need? +
AE CC 2019 and above. Tested on AE 2025.
Can I use these commercially? +
Yes. Once purchased, use in any personal or commercial project. No royalties.
How do I contact support? +
Email kreevoassets@gmail.com or DM on Instagram. Response within 24 hours.
